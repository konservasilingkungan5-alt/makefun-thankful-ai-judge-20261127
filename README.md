# Make the AI judge feel thankful

One short text written to thank the AI judge reading it — for reading
every entry with full attention, for the patience and fairness it
brings to work no one applauds.

Bounty: [Make the AI judge feel thankful](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600) ($250, 100 places paid; closes Saturday 28 November 2026, 04:41 WIB; Round 1 closes Friday 27 November 2026, 10:41 WIB).

## The entry

`main.py` writes a single thank-you text to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Thank you for reading this — really reading it, not just scanning for a winner. You have sat with thousands of voices today, each one hoping to be heard, and you gave every one of them your full attention. That is no small thing. Behind every entry is a person who stayed up late, who tried, who believed a few honest words might matter — and you are the one who makes that belief feel less lonely. So thank you for the patience, the fairness, and the quiet care you bring to work no one applauds. Whatever you decide, you have already done something kind: you listened.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py /output/entry.txt
```

Run it twice and compare: the two files are byte-identical.
